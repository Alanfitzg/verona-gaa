The Tocatì Festival

Tocatì — “tòca-a-tì”, your turn — is Verona's international festival of street games. Since 2003 it has turned the city into a free, open celebration of traditional play from around the world.

The club

A community, first

Verona GAA is a community first — and a sports club that opens the door to international travel, new friends and the craic. Beginners welcome. Social members welcome.

  • Community first Social members, families and friends — everyone belongs, on and off the pitch.
